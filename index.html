Free 3D mockups from your screenshots

Plinth turns screenshots into studio-lit 3D mockups. Choose a phone, tablet, laptop, browser window or card. It is free to use, with no account required.

How to create a mockup

  1. Choose, drop or paste a PNG, JPG or WebP screenshot.
  2. Pick a device and a ready-made look. Adjust the angle, lighting, background and output shape.
  3. Choose an export size, prepare the PNG, then select Download PNG.

Scene links share the settings, never your uploaded image.

Which mockup should I choose?

Use a phone or tablet for an app screen, a laptop or browser window for a website, or a card to show an image on its own. The devices are generic models.

How do I keep the whole screenshot visible?

Choose Fit image to show the whole screenshot. Fill screen fills the device screen and may crop the edges. New images start with Fill screen.

At 2× or 3×, both dimensions are multiplied by that number. For example, 16:9 at 2× is 3840 × 2160 pixels. Available sizes depend on your device's graphics limits; a larger size is not supported on every device.

Plinth downloads still PNG images. It does not export video.

What does a shared scene link include?

A scene link includes the device, pose, lighting, background and output settings. When someone opens it, they see the demo image and can add their own screenshot. To share your finished mockup, download and send the PNG.

What browser does Plinth need?

The interactive studio requires JavaScript and WebGL 2. It has desktop and mobile layouts; the export sizes available depend on the browser and graphics hardware.

Frequently asked questions

Is Plinth free? Do I need an account?

You can create and download PNG mockups for free, without signing up.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Your image stays in your browser.

Can I export a transparent PNG?

Yes. Choose Transparent in the Background control, then export your PNG. The checkerboard is only a preview and is not included in the file.

What do the 1×, 2× and 3× export sizes mean?

They increase the PNG's pixel dimensions, independently of your screen size. If a size is unavailable, choose a smaller one. Your PNG keeps the size you selected.
