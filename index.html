Screenshot studio

Free 3D mockups from your screenshots

Plinth turns screenshots into studio-lit 3D mockups. Choose a phone, tablet, laptop, browser window or card. It is free to use, with no account required.

How to create a mockup

  1. Choose, drop or paste a PNG, JPG or WebP screenshot.
  2. Pick a device and a ready-made look. Adjust the angle, lighting, background and output shape.
  3. Choose an export size, prepare the PNG, then select Download PNG.

Scene links share the settings, never your uploaded image.

Frequently asked questions

Is Plinth free? Do I need an account?

You can create and download PNG mockups for free, without signing up.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Your image stays in your browser.

Can I export a transparent PNG?

Yes. Choose Transparent in the Background control, then export your PNG. The checkerboard is only a preview and is not included in the file.

What do the 1×, 2× and 3× export sizes mean?

They increase the PNG's pixel dimensions, independently of your screen size. If a size is unavailable, choose a smaller one. Your PNG keeps the size you selected.
