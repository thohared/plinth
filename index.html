Free 3D mockups from your screenshots

Plinth turns screenshots into studio-lit 3D mockups for portfolios, app presentations and social posts. Choose a generic phone, tablet, laptop, browser window or card. It is free to use, with no account required.

How to create a mockup

  1. Choose, drop or paste a PNG, JPG or WebP screenshot.
  2. Pick a device and a ready-made look. Adjust the angle, lighting, background and output shape.
  3. Choose an export size, prepare the PNG, then select Download PNG.

Your image stays in your browser. Scene links share the settings, never your uploaded image. PNG export supports transparent backgrounds; available export sizes depend on your device.
