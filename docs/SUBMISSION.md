## Short description (under 200 characters)

Turn screenshots into studio-lit 3D device mockups. Pick a view, lighting and background, then export PNG. Free, no account, and your image stays in your browser.

## Longer description

Plinth is a browser-based screenshot studio with five generic device formats,
four lighting scenes, named angles and manual Free view. Add your screenshot,
adjust the framing and background, then export a PNG at 1×, 2× or 3× when your
device supports the selected dimensions. Transparent backgrounds are supported.
Scene links share settings without including your image. No account is needed,
and uploaded images stay in the browser. This version exports still images;
motion preview and video export are not available.
